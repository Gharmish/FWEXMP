## Design Context

### Users

Gharmish primarily serves Arabic-first Saudi domestic travelers discovering and booking authentic experiences in Abha and the wider Aseer region. Hosts are vetted local individuals and tourism companies; internal operators manage curation, bookings, payouts, disputes, and marketplace quality. Guests need to confidently find a suitable experience, understand availability and policies, book and pay, and retrieve trustworthy post-booking information.

### Brand Personality

Calm, confident, and culturally rooted. The product should feel premium without pretension, restrained rather than markety, and like a knowledgeable local host introducing a friend to Aseer.

### Aesthetic Direction

An Arabic-first, editorial marketplace with Apple-like restraint and a strong sense of Aseeri place. The established Gharmish palette, Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans Arabic typography, natural local photography, generous space, and shadowless hairline-separated surfaces define the visual system. The interface should avoid loud discount-marketplace conventions, generic card-grid repetition, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, and stock imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Make trust and booking clarity more prominent than promotion.
2. Express Aseeri identity through authentic content, photography, language, and the established brand palette.
3. Use deliberate restraint: clear hierarchy, few competing actions, generous rhythm, and no ornamental UI noise.
4. Treat Arabic, RTL behavior, readable type, and inclusive interaction as first-class product requirements.
5. Keep guest, host, and admin workflows operationally truthful: every status, deadline, price, policy, and communication promise must match the underlying system.
