## 0. Executive verdict & scorecard

The transactional core is **excellent** — derived (never-decremented) inventory under row locks,
snapshotted policy/commission/VAT so history is never restated, gateway-as-truth settlement with
conditional-UPDATE arbiters, append-only payment/wallet ledgers, idempotency at every seam. The
localization/compliance layer is the strongest-audited area: perfect 2,654-key catalog parity,
Latin digits on 40/40 `Intl` sites, textbook receipt-vs-tax-invoice gating.

The operational layer is immature in four specific ways:

1. **Rigorous about state and money; almost blind to time and people.** Approval clocks aren't
   clamped to event start; no-shows don't exist; suspending a host strands paid guests; requests
   expire with no host nudge.
2. **The admin console solves the platform's problems (settlement, VAT, moderation), not a support
   agent's.** 4 of 8 common tickets need raw SQL.
3. **Alerting is a monoculture** — one unmonitored Resend inbox; queues that are pages someone
   must remember to open.
4. **The demand funnel is dark and unworked.** Two analytics events total; Arabic search broken
   for real Arabic input; the highest-intent signals (saves, abandoned holds, zero-result
   searches) trigger no action.

## 3. Domain A — Guest booking lifecycle

**Architecture:** two orthogonal enums (lifecycle × payment) with a single transition map +
shared executor (`features/bookings/lib/transitions.ts:38-46`, `transition-executor.ts:67-269`).
Instant: insert `confirmed` + 30-min payment hold under `FOR UPDATE`. Request: insert `pending`

- 24h `approvalDeadline`; approval opens a payment window. One maintenance cron (12 passes)
  expires/releases/reconciles/reminds/completes.

**Strengths:** capacity is _derived_ (`sum(partySize)` over active statuses) — no counter to
corrupt; `holdStillCounts()` frees seats at SQL-evaluation time, independent of cron latency;
one lock anchor shared by create/approve/reschedule → no double-booking race; settlement is
idempotent across return-route/webhook/cron with amount re-assertion and cancel-during-3DS
auto-refund; booking-creation idempotency has three layers (client UUID, replay fast path,
constraint-name-matched backstop).
